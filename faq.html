frequently asked questions

what is infiniware?

infiniware is a small software ecosystem built around quiet interfaces, structural clarity, and privacy-first products. the website, the projects, and the community area are meant to feel like parts of one system.

why is the entire site lowercase?

lowercase is part of the voice. it keeps the tone quieter and less performative. the only deliberate exception is the red "I" in the wordmark.

what is al-muslim?

al-muslim is one of the current infiniware projects. the direction is consistent across releases: no ads, privacy first, and fast where it matters.

is there a linux version?

yes. al-muslim for linux is live as version 1.0v and includes a standalone qt desktop app plus a kde plasma widget. current public repo languages include qml, shell, c++, and cmake.

where can i download it?

the products page carries the current download action and source code links for public releases. the linux project currently links to the provided tar.gz artifact and the official github repository.

does infiniware use ads or sell data?

the current direction is no ads and no personal-data resale. the privacy policy page explains this in more detail, along with the limited data needed for account and community features.

how can i get help or contact the project?

use info@infiniware.bid for general contact and support@infiniware.bid for technical help. you can also follow the github, mastodon, and discord links in the footer.
