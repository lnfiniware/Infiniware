privacy policy

this policy explains what infiniware collects, what it does not collect, and how data is handled across the website, community features, and current projects.

1. what we collect

if you create an account, we may process your email address, authentication details, and the profile information you choose to provide. if you post in the community area, we store the content you submit so it can be shown back to you and others in that space.

2. what we do not collect

we do not run advertising trackers. we do not sell personal data. we do not build user profiles for third-party marketing. for al-muslim, device location used for prayer time calculations is intended to stay on-device for the experience itself.

3. how data is used

data is used to provide core functionality: account access, community posting, and the visible operation of the service. we try to keep the data surface small and tied to direct product function rather than analytics theater.

4. retention and deletion

account and community data may remain available until you request deletion or the data is removed through normal product controls. if you need help with deletion, contact support@infiniware.bid.

5. security

we use reasonable technical measures to protect the service and stored data, but no internet service can promise absolute security. you should also keep your own device, browser, and credentials secure.

6. updates to this policy

this page may change as the website and projects evolve. when it changes, the intent remains the same: collect less, explain more clearly, and avoid turning users into a data product.
